## Branding and watermark requirements

- **Every published Vance image must display the supplied “THE SERAPHS CRYPT” gothic winged logo watermark.**
- Position the watermark at the lower-right, proportional to the image and partially transparent, while preserving the portrait.
- The prepared ZIP already contains all ten watermarked images.
- Retain the two reusable brand assets: `seraphs-crypt-watermark-original.jpeg` and `seraphs-crypt-watermark-transparent.png`.
- Future character art published under Seraph's Crypt should also be watermarked before uploading.
- Do not accidentally replace watermarked images with unwatermarked originals.
